FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Quick answers about booking, drop-off, pricing, and payment. Don't see your question? Call, text, or message us on Instagram.

Booking & drop-off

How do I book an appointment?

Tap any Book Now button on the site. It opens our online calendar, where you can pick an open time. You can also call or text (208) 440-7597 or email detailautoglow208@gmail.com.

Do you come to me, or do I bring my vehicle to you?

We work on a drop-off basis. You bring your vehicle to us at your appointment time and pick it up when it's done.

Where do I drop off my vehicle?

We'll send you the drop-off address when we confirm your appointment. If you have questions before booking, call or text (208) 440-7597.

Do you take walk-ins?

Services are by appointment only, so we can give every vehicle the time it needs. Book online or reach out to set up a time.

Should I take anything out of my vehicle first?

Yes, please remove personal items and valuables before drop-off, especially for an interior detail. It helps us clean every surface and keeps your belongings safe.

Pricing & payment

How much does it cost?

Interior Detail: cars from $55, SUVs from $65, pickup trucks from $100.

Exterior Hand Wash: cars from $40, SUVs from $45, pickup trucks from $50.

See the full Services & Prices page for what's included.

Why do prices say "starting at"?

The final price depends on your vehicle's size and condition. An interior with excessive hair, pet fur, sand, or dirt may have an extra cleaning fee starting at $20. An exterior with excessive mud or dirt may have an extra fee starting at $30. If a fee applies, we'll let you know.

What forms of payment do you accept?

We accept cash, Venmo, and card.

Services

What's included in an Interior Detail?

Floor mats, full vacuum, trunk vacuum, dashboard cleaning, door jambs, interior windows and mirrors, and stain spot treatment.

What's included in an Exterior Hand Wash?

A thorough hand wash of your vehicle's entire exterior.

Can I get both an interior detail and a hand wash?

Yes. Let us know when you book and we'll take care of both.

Who does the work?

Auto Glow is run by Wyatt, a young entrepreneur, with the support of his family. He's still learning and growing his skills, and every job gets real care and attention to detail. Thanks for supporting a local, family-run business!
